**Assessment:** Echoray already implements services/experiences, bookings, dated event tickets, QR admission, monetary gift cards and manual orders. The principal missing feature is an **open-date voucher for a specific experience**, with recipient, allowed location(s), ~365-day validity, branded PDF and eventual redemption. Monetary gift cards and dated admission passes do not supply that lifecycle.
